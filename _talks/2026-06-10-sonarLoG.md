This talk, which was selected for an Oral Presentation at the LoG Italian Meetup 2026, shows how the wave equation on Graphs can be used to enhance the Long-Range capabilities of Message Passing Neural Networks on Graphs. Starting from the physical intuitions, the presentation gradually develops the full model and its extension from static graphs to dynamic ones. Taken from the work with Alessio Gravina at University of Pisa and other colleagues.
